How it plays

Tilt to play

Tip your iPhone left and right to slide the paddle under your Bro. Where they land sends them left or right. Tap for their special move.

Rescue the Bros

Start with Pip and fly through five galaxies. At the end of each one, pop a bubble to free a new Bro for your crew.

A new challenge every day

Everyone gets the same Daily Challenge. Chase a high score, then share it and see who bounced best.

Meet the crew

Every Bro bounces their own way.
