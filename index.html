How it plays

Tilt to play

Tip your iPhone or iPad left and right to slide the paddle under your Bro. Where they land sends them left or right. Tap for their special move.

Rescue the Bros

Start with Pip and bounce through galaxy after galaxy. Pop the bubbles to free new Bros, then take on a boss at the end of every chapter.

A new challenge every day

Everyone gets the same Daily Challenge. Chase a high score, then share it and see who bounced best.

Black holes, meteors and more

Dodge asteroids, ride solar winds, slip through wormholes, bounce on icy paddles and light up the dark.

Upgrades and Gold Suits

Collect stardust to power up every Bro's move. Earn all the stars with a Bro to win their Gold Suit.

Secrets

There's more out there than the levels show. Some say a starry Bro appears for the true completionists.
